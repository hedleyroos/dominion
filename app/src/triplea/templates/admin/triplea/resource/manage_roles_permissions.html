Roles and permissions mapping for {{ object }}

You may adjust the roles and permissions mapping for every resource. Inherit means that the roles for a particular permission are inherited from the resource's ancestors and also its domain ancestors. Unticking Inherit means this resource is responsible for the roles for a particular permission. Child resources inherit their roles and permissions mapping from this resource by default.

It is normal for Inherit to be ticked and the boxes to the right to not be ticked. Ticking these boxes augments the inherited permissions.
